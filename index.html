Why the Gladiator JT Needs Its Own Seat Covers

The Jeep Gladiator JT shares its platform with the Wrangler JL, but Jeep made meaningful changes to the seating architecture when they added the pickup bed. Here's what's different and why it matters for seat cover selection:

Front seats: The Gladiator JT's front seats have longer rails than the Wrangler JL to accommodate the extended cab footwell. Seat covers that use under-seat strap anchoring may not reach correctly if they're patterned on the JL. Custom-fit JT covers anchor at the correct points.

Rear bench: This is the biggest difference. The Wrangler JL has a fold-and-tumble rear seat that folds completely flat for cargo. The Gladiator JT has a conventional truck-style rear bench that folds forward but does not tumble. The bench cushion profile, mounting points, and seatback dimensions are different. A Wrangler JL rear cover will not fit properly on the JT bench.

Trim variation: The JT was offered in Sport, Sport S, Overland, Rubicon, Mojave, and Willys trims from 2020–2026. The seat base design is consistent across trims — but some premium trims have additional bolstering or seating sensors. Custom-fit covers from Bartact account for these variations.

JT production span: The Jeep Gladiator JT launched for the 2020 model year and continued through 2026 with minimal seat architecture changes. A quality custom-fit cover for 2020 will work correctly on a 2026 model.
